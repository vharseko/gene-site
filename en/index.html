    Where we search

    Every archive, database, library and website where we have looked for the family: what the source is, how to work with it and what we found there. A number opens the document; “Search log” lists every search in that source, successful or not.

    Restored photographs

    Copies of old family photos with the creases and stains removed — in black and white and colourised. The colours are the restorer’s guess; the original remains the document, and you can open it by its number.
